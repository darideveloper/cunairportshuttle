# Capability: English Translations for Destination Cards

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: English Translation for Destination Cards
The English translation file MUST include full descriptions for all destination cards in the main destinations page.

#### Scenario: Translate Tulum Description
- **Given** the Spanish description: "Tulum, ciudad situada en la Riviera Maya, se encuentra aproximadamente a 2 horas en coche de Cancún. Conocida por sus impresionantes playas, sitios arqueológicos históricos e increíbles hoteles en la selva, Tulum es un destino de visita obligada."
- **When** the page is rendered in English
- **Then** the description MUST be: "Tulum, a city located in the Riviera Maya, is approximately 2 hours by car from Cancun. Known for its stunning beaches, historic archaeological sites, and incredible jungle hotels, Tulum is a must-visit destination."

#### Scenario: Translate Playa del Carmen Description
- **Given** the Spanish description: "Nuestros servicios de transporte y taxi te llevarán a cualquier hotel, casa vacacional o Airbnb en Playa del Carmen, el vibrante corazón de la Riviera Maya. ¡Con nosotros, su aventura está a la vuelta de la esquina!"
- **When** the page is rendered in English
- **Then** the description MUST be: "Our transportation and taxi services will take you to any hotel, vacation home, or Airbnb in Playa del Carmen, the vibrant heart of the Riviera Maya. With us, your adventure is just around the corner!"

#### Scenario: Translate Cancun Hotel Zone Description
- **Given** the Spanish description: "La zona hotelera de Cancún es el principal destino de la ciudad, con hoteles de 4 y 5 estrellas que ofrecen las mejores vistas del Mar Caribe y la experiencia vacacional más inolvidable que pueda imaginar."
- **When** the page is rendered in English
- **Then** the description MUST be: "The Cancun Hotel Zone is the city's main destination, with 4 and 5-star hotels offering the best views of the Caribbean Sea and the most unforgettable vacation experience you can imagine."
