# Proposal: Rebrand Private Airport Transfer Page

## Why
The current private transfer page uses outdated branding ("Cancun Airport Shuttle by Go Transfers") and generic service descriptions. This change aligns the `/private-airport-transfer-cancun` (EN) and `/es/transporte-privado-cancun` (ES) pages with the new "Cun Airport Shuttle" brand guidelines and updated service descriptions.

## What Changes
- **Brand Update:** Replace all instances of "Cancun Airport Shuttle by Go Transfers" with "Cun Airport Shuttle."
- **SEO Optimization:** Update Meta Titles and Descriptions for both English and Spanish.
- **Service Benefits:** Introduce a specific list of 7 "Includes" items for the private transfer service.
- **Pricing Table:** Update headers and rows to match the new rates ($29.99 USD One Way / $59.99 USD Round Trip).
- **Testimonials:** Update review text to demonstrate current reliability.
- **FAQ Refinement:** Clarify passenger capacity and booking processes.
- **Luxury Card Fix:** Correct the Spanish title of the Luxury card in the Pricing Section.
- **H1 vs Title:** Distinguish between technical metadata title and on-page H1 header.
