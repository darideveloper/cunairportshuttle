**Last Updated: April 2026**

These are the terms and conditions for **Cun Airport Shuttle** (by Go Transfers) services in the Riviera Maya. In compliance with **Mexico’s Federal Law of Protection of Personal Data in Possession of Individuals**, we are responsible for the processing of your personal data.

## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect information from you when you fill out a form to make a reservation or contact us. The information provided must be true; **Cun Airport Shuttle** is not responsible if the user provides incorrect or false information.

**Data required for logistics and booking:**

* **Full Name & Gender.**  
* **Email Address & Physical Address.**  
* **Phone Numbers** (Home, office, or cell).  
* **Flight Information** (Airline and flight number).  
* **Financial Data:** Credit card number, cardholder name, expiration date, and security code (CVV) for booking completion.

## 2. How Do We Process Your Payments?

To ensure maximum security, all transactions are handled by industry-leading third-party providers:

* **Openpay:** All transactions are validated using the **3D Secure** protocol, requiring additional verification from your bank.  
* **PayPal:** Information is processed directly within their secure system.  
* **Security Note:** All sensitive information is transmitted via **Secure Socket Layer (SSL)** technology. **Cun Airport Shuttle** does **not store** credit card billing data or CVV codes on our servers.

## 3. Use of the Requested Information

Any information we collect may be used to:

* Speed up the vehicle reservation process and provide the requested services.  
* Monitor, update, cancel, or confirm your contracted service.  
* Personalize your experience and improve our customer service.  
* Send periodic emails regarding your booking or service updates.  
* Conduct surveys on preferences and evaluate service quality.

## 4. Data Transfer (National and International)

Your personal data may be shared with business partners, affiliates, suppliers, and financial institutions (American Express, MasterCard, or VISA) to confirm reservations. **Cun Airport Shuttle** reserves the right to disclose information to authorities when legally required to safeguard public interest or investigate fraudulent acts, as per **Article 37** of Mexico’s Federal Law.

## 5. Cookies and Third-Party Tools

We use cookies to recognize your browser and remember preferences (like language). We also use **Google Analytics** to analyze user interaction and Google advertising cookies to show relevant ads. You can disable these in your browser settings at any time.

## 6. Exercise of ARCO Rights (Access, Rectification, Cancellation, Opposition)

You have the right to access, rectify, cancel, or oppose the treatment of your personal data. To exercise this right, submit a written request in Spanish or English to:

* **Email:** **bookings@gotransfers.us**

Include your full name, a copy of your official ID, and a clear description of the data you wish to modify. We will provide an answer within **20 working days**.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Notice

**Cun Airport Shuttle** reserves the right to update this Privacy Notice at any time. Modifications become effective the moment they are published on [cunairportshuttle.com](https://cunairportshuttle.com). Your use of our website constitutes acceptance of these terms.
